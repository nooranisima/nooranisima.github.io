I am a PhD student in AI/ML at the University of Pennsylvania. My research focuses on uncertainty quantification for modern machine learning systems, including generative models (LLMs), and on how to design principled, reliable uncertainty estimates when humans and AI systems interact to make decisions.

Research

I am broadly interested in reliable AI, with a focus on:

  • Conformal prediction and distribution-free uncertainty quantification.
  • Uncertainty for generative models and large language models.
  • Human–AI collaborative decision making and evaluation.
  • Sequential / online settings and robustness to distribution shift.
